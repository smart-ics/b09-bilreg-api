# 01-context.md — Tata Rekening

## FEATURE NAME

Tata Rekening

---

# UBIQUITOUS LANGUAGE

Three concepts must not be conflated:

| Term | Kind | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Tata Rekening** | Bounded context | Hospital financial responsibility capability — verification, adjustment, allocation, finalization, carry-over orchestration |
| **Tata Rekening** | Aggregate | Financial authority for **one registration**; owns the Billing Set and lifecycle state |
| **Verifikator** | Actor | Hospital user who executes the Tata Rekening workflow (verification, adjustment, finalization, carry-over selection) |
| **TrsBill** | Entity | Individual financial charge within a Tata Rekening aggregate |

Bounded context and aggregate **share the business name** Tata Rekening. No alternate aggregate name (e.g. FinancialCase) is introduced — the term is already understood by hospital users.

Wording guide:

- **Verifikator** verifies, adjusts, selects carry-over, and submits finalization input.
- **Tata Rekening aggregate** validates, enforces invariants, transitions lifecycle state, and records financial responsibility.
- **Tata Rekening bounded context** is the scope of capabilities and integration described in this documentation.

Avoid phrasing such as "Tata Rekening performs verification" when the actor is meant — prefer "Verifikator performs verification; the Tata Rekening aggregate validates."

---

# PURPOSE

Tata Rekening is the hospital **financial responsibility domain** for patient registrations.

Its responsibility is:

```text
recognize
allocate
verify
adjust
finalize
settle
project
```

patient financial responsibility generated by operational subsystems.

Tata Rekening exists because:

```text
Operational Truth
≠
Financial Truth
```

Operational subsystems record:

```text
what operationally happened
```

Tata Rekening records:

```text
what financially must be recognized, allocated, and finalized
```

Legacy tables such as `ta_trs_billing` are **persistence artifacts** used to implement this domain — not the domain itself.

---

# WHY TATA REKENING EXISTS

Hospital operational workflow and financial workflow evolve differently.

Operational subsystems:
- focus on workflow execution,
- own medical/operational history,
- may change operational structure over time.

Financial responsibility requires:
- stable pricing snapshot,
- stable accounting projection,
- settlement authority,
- receivable consistency across Rawat Jalan and Rawat Inap.

Without separation:
- pricing becomes inconsistent,
- accounting projection becomes unstable,
- operational subsystems become tightly coupled to billing,
- financial reconciliation becomes difficult.

Tata Rekening separates:
- operational ownership,
- financial ownership.

---

# RELATED BOUNDED CONTEXTS

Tata Rekening sits in the middle of the hospital financial pipeline.

```text
Charge Source
    ↓
Tata Rekening
    ↓
Cashier
    ↓
Accounting
```

**PasienBalance** is a neighboring bounded context that Tata Rekening **consumes** but does **not** own:

```text
PasienBalance
    ↓
Current Outstanding Receivable
    ↓
consumed by
    ↓
Tata Rekening
```

| Bounded Context | Owns | Relationship to Tata Rekening |
|---|---|---|
| Charge Source | operational charge transactions (tindakan, room, pharmacy, transport, etc.) | supplies charge requests; Tata Rekening recognizes financial charge |
| **Tata Rekening** | financial truth for a registration | central authority |
| **PasienBalance** | patient-level current outstanding receivables (one entry per unsettled RegId) | consumed at registration open for carry-over selection; updated when Tata Rekening consumes entries |
| Cashier | payment collection and settlement execution | receives finalized, cashier-ready responsibility from Tata Rekening |
| Accounting | journal generation and general ledger | consumes accounting-ready projection; remains external |
| Tarif | pricing policy and tariff master | supplies immutable pricing snapshot at charge time |

Ownership boundaries:

- Charge Source owns **operational truth** and operational correction.
- PasienBalance owns **current outstanding receivable state** per patient — not carry-over decisions.
- The **Tata Rekening bounded context** owns **registration-scoped financial truth**, allocation rules, finalization invariants, and settlement orchestration.
- Cashier owns **cash collection**.
- Accounting owns **journal posting**.

---

# AGGREGATE SCOPE

```text
Bounded Context     Tata Rekening
Aggregate           Tata Rekening   (one per registration)
Actor               Verifikator
```

Tata Rekening aggregate lifecycle exists at **registration scope** — not individual charge-row scope.

Because:
- payer allocation is registration-based,
- settlement is registration-based,
- close/finalization is registration-based,
- financial responsibility belongs to registration lifecycle.

The same financial lifecycle applies to **Rawat Jalan** and **Rawat Inap**. Operational triggers differ (e.g. outpatient completion vs inpatient operational discharge), but the financial lifecycle is shared.

Current implementation may map the aggregate to `TrsBillingRegister` / `TataRekeningModel` — implementation naming only; the business aggregate remains **Tata Rekening**.

# PERSISTENCE STRATEGY

Tata Rekening preserves existing legacy financial tables:

| Table | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| `ta_trs_billing` | authoritative billing charge row |
| `ta_trs_billing2` | financial distribution projection |
| `ta_registrasi3` | payer allocation authority |

Additional orchestration table may exist:

| Table | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| `BILRG_TrsBillingRegister` | registration lifecycle orchestration |

Why:
- legacy tables were designed as financial rows,
- not lifecycle aggregates,
- not orchestration boundaries.

The orchestration table exists to:
- clarify aggregate boundary,
- simplify lifecycle management,
- simplify finalize/reopen logic,
- improve concurrency handling,
- reduce modeling ambiguity.

Legacy table names appear **only** in persistence and implementation sections. Business documentation refers to Tata Rekening, TrsBill, and related domain concepts.

# CORE PRINCIPLE

Tata Rekening is:

# Current-State Financial Responsibility Ledger

NOT:
- immutable event sourcing ledger,
- full accounting journal engine,
- distributed financial orchestration system.

Principles (unchanged):

| Principle | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Legacy Compatibility First | preserve existing billing/accounting workflow |
| Pricing Snapshot Immutable | historical pricing must not change |
| Accounting Snapshot Immutable | historical accounting mapping must not change |
| Mutable Before Finalization | billing may still change before financial finalization |
| Financial Freeze After Payment | paid billing becomes immutable |
| Source Owns Operational History | operational correction belongs to source subsystem |
| Delete → Regenerate Projection | billing2 projection is derived, not immutable history |
| Billing2 Is Projection | `ta_trs_billing2` is financial decomposition, not event history |
| Accounting Remains External | journal generation stays in Accounting bounded context |
| Compatibility Preferred Over Architectural Purity | operational stability over greenfield redesign |


# FINANCIAL LIFECYCLE

```text
OPEN
    ↓
CLOSE BILL
    ↓
FINALIZED
    ↓
LUNAS (PAID)
```

Rawat Jalan and Rawat Inap share this lifecycle. **Inpatient operational discharge** and **outpatient completion** are operational events that may trigger Close Bill — they do not define the financial lifecycle itself. Financial finalization is a separate, lifecycle-neutral step performed by the Verifikator after verification and allocation.

---

## OPEN

Operational billing still mutable.

Allowed:
- add/remove charge,
- allocation recalculation,
- billing regeneration,
- financial adjustment via additional charge.

---

## CLOSE BILL

Operational freeze point.

Triggered by operational events such as:
- inpatient operational discharge,
- outpatient completion,
- other operational closing events.

Close Bill freezes operational charge generation; it does not finalize financial responsibility.

No more operational charge generation allowed.

---

## FINALIZED

Financial finalization completed by the Verifikator workflow.

The Tata Rekening aggregate records:
- financial verification outcome,
- financial responsibility allocation locked,
- cashier-ready state,
- accounting-ready projection stable.

Billing becomes:
- allocation-locked,
- reopen restricted (unless cancel finalization allowed).

---

## LUNAS (PAID)

Settlement completed via Cashier.

Billing becomes financially frozen.

---

# CARRY OVER

Carry over is a first-class business capability connecting PasienBalance and Tata Rekening.

```text
Open Registration
    ↓
Load PasienBalance
    ↓
Outstanding Entries (one per unsettled RegId)
    ↓
Verifikator selects carry-over entries
    ↓
Tata Rekening aggregate records new financial responsibility
    ↓
PasienBalance updated (entry consumed or adjusted)
```

PasienBalance never performs carry-over itself. The Verifikator selects which outstanding receivables to carry over; the Tata Rekening aggregate materializes financial responsibility and enforces invariants.

# FINANCIAL DISTRIBUTION SEMANTICS

`ta_trs_billing2` represents:

# Financial Distribution Projection

NOT:
- event sourcing history,
- accounting journal,
- immutable mutation stream.

Projection rows may represent:
- tarif component distribution,
- payer allocation distribution,
- receivable ownership transfer,
- accounting-ready decomposition.

Example semantics:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `FN_TRS_P` | receivable acquisition |
| `FN_TRS_N` | receivable release / ownership transfer |

NOT:
- debit/credit,
- positive/negative accounting number.

---

# FINANCIAL ADJUSTMENT

The Tata Rekening bounded context owns **financial adjustment** as a business capability. The Verifikator presents adjustments through workflow; the aggregate processes and records them — it does not invent operational corrections at the Charge Source.

Examples:
- manual charge,
- manual correction,
- waive,
- subsidy,
- carry-over adjustment,
- billing correction.

Adjustment typically materializes as additional TrsBill entries or allocation recalculation while Tata Rekening remains OPEN or before finalization locks responsibility.

---

# INTEGRATION PRINCIPLE

Subsystem integration pattern:

```text
Subsystem (Charge Source)
→ Charge Request
→ Tata Rekening
```

NOT:

```text
Subsystem Aggregate
→ manipulate billing persistence directly
```

Charge Source owns:
- operational truth,
- workflow history,
- operational correction.

The **Tata Rekening bounded context** owns:

- financial receivable recognition,
- allocation rules,
- finalization invariants,
- settlement orchestration,
- accounting projection source.

---

# WHY COMPOSITE CHARGE EXISTS

Operational atomicity is NOT always equal to financial atomicity.

One operational workflow may produce:
- multiple financial charges,
- bundled pricing,
- package billing,
- compressed commercial representation.

Composite charge is:
- valid business pattern,
- not legacy mistake.

---

# ACCOUNTING POSITIONING

Tata Rekening is NOT the accounting engine.

The bounded context provides:

# Accounting Projection Source

because:
- accounting requires historical consistency,
- journal generation is asynchronous,
- accounting workflow already exists in legacy system.
