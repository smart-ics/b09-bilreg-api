# 02-domain.md — TRSBILLING

# 1. Purpose

TRSBILLING is the core hospital financial receivable domain.

This domain is responsible for:

```text
billing recognition
financial responsibility allocation
billing settlement
accounting projection
```

for all operational activities that generate patient financial charges.

TRSBILLING is NOT:

- clinical workflow domain,
- medical procedure domain,
- inventory domain,
- general accounting domain,
- payment gateway domain.

TRSBILLING is:

# Hospital Operational Financial Receivable Ledger

designed with:

```text
legacy compatibility first
```

while improving:
- pricing consistency,
- financial traceability,
- subsystem decoupling,
- accounting projection quality.

---

# 2. Core Responsibility

| Responsibility | Description |
|---|---|
| Billing Recognition | Recognize financial receivable generated by operational activity |
| Pricing Snapshot | Store historical pricing at transaction time |
| Accounting Snapshot | Store historical accounting mapping snapshot |
| Responsibility Allocation | Allocate billing responsibility to payer/guarantor |
| Billing Settlement | Handle receivable settlement process |
| Accounting Projection | Provide accounting-ready journal source |
| Financial Traceability | Preserve traceability to operational source transaction |

---

# 4. Important Architectural Principle

## Operational Truth ≠ Financial Truth

Operational subsystem records:

```text
what operationally happened
```

TRSBILLING records:

```text
what financially must be billed
```

Examples:

| Operational | Financial |
|---|---|
| medical procedure | receivable |
| drug dispensing | billing charge |
| room occupancy | patient receivable |
| transport usage | financial charge |

Operational subsystem remains the authority for:
- workflow history,
- operational activity,
- medical execution.

TRSBILLING becomes the authority for:
- financial receivable,
- pricing snapshot,
- billing settlement,
- accounting projection source.

---

# 5. Aggregate Structure

TRSBILLING consists of one primary aggregate:

# BillingCharge Aggregate

---

## Aggregate Root

### `ta_trs_billing`

Represents:

```text
authoritative operational financial charge
```

Stores:

- registration reference,
- tarif identity,
- pricing snapshot,
- source transaction snapshot,
- billing amount,
- operational context,
- financial responsibility context.

`ta_trs_billing` remains the authoritative existing legacy billing table.

---

## Child Collection

### `ta_trs_billing2`

Represents:

# Billing Financial Allocation Entries

and:

# Accounting Projection Source Entries

Stores:

- financial allocation distribution,
- payer allocation,
- settlement allocation,
- tarif component allocation,
- accounting account snapshot,
- billing distribution detail.

`ta_trs_billing2` is NOT:
- immutable event stream,
- full accounting journal,
- event sourcing timeline,
- standalone financial ledger.

Instead, it acts as:

```text
financial allocation and accounting projection detail
```

attached to a billing charge.

---

# 6. Aggregate Invariants

## Invariant 1 — Billing Authority

`ta_trs_billing` is the authoritative billing amount.

All allocation entries must remain consistent with the billing amount.

---

## Invariant 2 — Pricing Snapshot Integrity

Pricing snapshot must remain historically consistent.

Changes in:
- tarif master,
- patient class,
- pricing policy,
- payer configuration

must NOT change historical billing pricing.

---

## Invariant 3 — Accounting Snapshot Integrity

Historical accounting mapping must remain unchanged after billing creation.

Changes in:
- COA mapping,
- tarif-account mapping,
- accounting configuration

must NOT alter historical accounting projection.

---

## Invariant 4 — Source Transaction Required

Every billing charge must trace back to operational source transaction.

Billing cannot exist without operational source reference.

---

## Invariant 5 — Mutable Before Finalization

Billing may still change before billing finalization/payment settlement.

Operational correction before finalization is allowed.

---

## Invariant 6 — Settlement Freeze

After payment settlement:

```text
billing becomes financially frozen
```

Further correction must be handled through finance/accounting process.

---

# 7. Billing Lifecycle

TRSBILLING uses pragmatic operational financial lifecycle.

---

## Stage 1 — Billing Recognition

Operational subsystem generates financial charge.

Examples:
- medical service,
- pharmacy transaction,
- room charge,
- transport service.

Billing:
- still editable,
- still adjustable,
- not yet financially finalized.

---

## Stage 2 — Billing Allocation

Financial responsibility allocation is applied.

Allocation authority comes from:

```text
ta_registrasi3
```

TRSBILLING performs:
- proportional allocation,
- component distribution,
- accounting-ready decomposition.

One billing may have:
- multiple payer allocation,
- multiple financial responsibility owner.

---

## Stage 3 — Billing Finalization

Billing becomes operationally finalized.

After finalization:
- operational modification becomes restricted,
- billing consistency becomes locked,
- accounting projection becomes stable.

---

## Stage 4 — Payment Settlement

Payment and receivable settlement occur.

Settlement may involve:
- cash payment,
- guarantor settlement,
- insurance responsibility,
- BPJS settlement.

After settlement:
- billing becomes financially frozen,
- further correction moves to finance/accounting process.

---

# 8. Reopen Rules

Billing may reopen only if:

| Condition | Allowed |
|---|---|
| no payment settlement | YES |
| already settled | NO |

If payment already exists:
- correction must occur in finance/accounting domain,
- not through operational billing modification.

---

# 9. Financial Allocation Semantics

Financial allocation represents:

```text
who is financially responsible
```

for a billing charge.

Allocation may include:
- patient,
- BPJS,
- insurance,
- corporate guarantor,
- mixed responsibility.

Allocation values are stored as:

```text
absolute currency value
```

instead of percentage-based responsibility.

Percentage calculation, if needed, must occur outside TRSBILLING.

---

# 10. Financial Atomicity

Financial atomicity differs based on billing category.

---

## Service Billing

Uses:

```text
FS_KD_DETIL_TARIF
```

because:
- service component is configurable,
- accounting mapping attaches to tarif component,
- billing decomposition is more dynamic.

---

## Drug / Inventory Billing

Uses:

```text
FS_KD_GRUP_REK
```

because:
- accounting grouping is more standardized,
- inventory accounting structure is more stable.

---

# 11. Accounting Projection Semantics

TRSBILLING is NOT a full accounting journal engine.

TRSBILLING acts as:

# accounting-ready projection source

because:
- billing stores accounting snapshot,
- accounting mapping is historically preserved,
- accounting journal may be generated asynchronously.

Accounting journal generation is performed by:
- external service,
- cronjob,
- accounting subsystem.

---

# 12. Why Accounting Snapshot Exists

Accounting snapshot exists to preserve:

# historical accounting consistency

Goals:

- accounting history remains stable,
- COA mapping changes do not alter historical billing,
- journal projection remains historically accurate.

---

# 13. Composite Charge Semantics

Operational atomicity is NOT always equal to financial atomicity.

TRSBILLING must support:
- composite charge,
- bundled charge,
- multi-component charge,
- workflow-compressed commercial representation.

Examples:
- surgery package,
- delivery package,
- grouped service package,
- legacy composite transaction.

---

# 14. Integration Boundary

External subsystem:

- MUST NOT manipulate billing allocation directly,
- MUST NOT own financial receivable ledger,
- MUST NOT depend on internal billing persistence structure.

Integration pattern:

```text
Subsystem
→ Charge Request
→ TRSBILLING
```

NOT:

```text
Subsystem Aggregate
→ manipulate Billing Aggregate directly
```

# 16. Legacy Compatibility Principle

TRSBILLING prioritizes compatibility with existing HIS billing structure.

Therefore:

- `ta_trs_billing` remains authoritative,
- `ta_trs_billing2` remains central allocation/detail table,
- existing cashier and billing workflow remain operational,
- accounting projection continues using existing billing structure,
- migration strategy prefers incremental enhancement instead of full redesign.

Additional tables may be introduced only when:
- existing structure cannot safely support new requirement,
- compatibility remains preserved,
- operational workflow remains unchanged.

Pattern:

Subsystem

---

# 18. Final Domain Positioning

TRSBILLING is:

# Hospital Operational Financial Receivable Ledger

that:

- receives financial recognition from operational subsystem,
- stores historical pricing and accounting snapshot,
- manages financial responsibility allocation,
- supports settlement process,
- generates accounting-ready projection,
- preserves operational financial consistency,
- and remains compatible with existing HIS billing architecture.
